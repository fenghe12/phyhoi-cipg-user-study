## 中文

**问题：**人物用修枝剪剪竹竿，整个交互的物理表现有多合理？

**人体动作与发力：**观察手对竹竿的支撑、另一手对剪柄的控制，以及双臂与躯干的协调。握持方式和剪切幅度可以不同。

**接触与执行：**剪刃闭合位置、竹竿接触部位和手部操作应相互对应。剪刀与竹竿的可见位置应连续。

**物体变化：**若竹竿出现压痕、弯曲或分离，观察这些变化是否与剪刃作用相联系。不规定必须一次剪断。

**可能后果：**若剪下的竹段或枝叶移动、下落或与其他物体碰撞，观察其运动及支撑关系。不要求所有后果出现。

5 分：实际剪切已呈现，人体、剪刀、竹竿和可见后续运动协调，没有明显物理异常。

4 分：主要剪切过程及人体、工具和竹竿的反应合理，仅有轻微局部异常。

3 分：剪柄操作、剪刃接触或竹竿变化中既有合理部分，也有影响关键过程的明显异常。

2 分：可辨认剪切尝试，但剪刀的关键作用与竹竿反应严重不符。

1 分：人体、剪刀与竹竿的可见过程整体严重不合理，或画面清楚显示没有执行剪切。

无法判断：仅有持物或准备，或剪刃与竹竿的实际交互始终不可见，无法评价剪切时选择此项。

## English

**Question:** How physically plausible is the overall interaction as the person cuts a bamboo pole with pruning shears?

**Body and effort:** Consider how one hand supports the bamboo, how the other controls the handles, and how the arms and torso coordinate. Different grips and ranges of motion are possible.

**Contact and execution:** The blade closure, contact point on the bamboo, and hand action should correspond. The visible positions of the shears and bamboo should remain continuous.

**Object changes:** If the bamboo is indented, bends, or separates, consider whether the changes relate to the blade action. Cutting through in one attempt is not required.

**Possible aftermath:** If a cut section or leaves move, fall, or collide with another object, consider their motion and support. These outcomes need not all occur.

5: Actual cutting is shown, and the person, shears, bamboo, and visible subsequent motion are coherent without obvious physical anomalies.

4: The main cutting process and the person, tool, and bamboo responses are plausible, with minor local anomalies.

3: Plausible handle action, blade contact, or bamboo changes coexist with clear anomalies affecting the key process.

2: A cutting attempt is recognizable, but the key action of the shears and the bamboo response seriously disagree.

1: The visible person, shears, and bamboo behave implausibly overall, or a clear view confirms that no cutting action occurs.

Unable to judge: Choose this option if the person only holds or prepares the objects, or the actual blade–bamboo interaction is never visible and cannot be evaluated.
